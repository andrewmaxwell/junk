Trash Dash - 2026 - Drive a garbage truck, haul stinky bags to the dump, and dodge the Soap Men

Arrows or WASD to drive. Bags lose value the longer they sit, gold bags are worth triple, and a full load of 6 doubles your haul. Soap Men chase you, scrub bags out of your truck, and leave slippery puddles behind. Ram them at full speed to send them flying. Two minutes on the clock.

Press 2 on the title screen for Truck vs Soap: one player drives the truck with the arrow keys, the other is a Soap Man on WASD who drops soap puddles with Q, E or Shift. Soap scores double the value of every bag it scrubs, plus points while the truck slides on its soap.
