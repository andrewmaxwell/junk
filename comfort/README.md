Walk Comfort - 2026 - How nice it is outside for a walk or a sit, right now and over the next week.

A single number for whether it's worth going outside, worked out from a proper thermal comfort model (UTCI, with sun and shade) plus rain, wind, UV, air quality and daylight. Switch between a 30 minute walk and an hour sitting in the shade, tap **Why this score?** to see what's costing it, and check the chart for the best window over the next seven days. Uses your location, or search for anywhere. Weather from Open-Meteo.

Tests: `node --test comfort/test/*.test.js`
