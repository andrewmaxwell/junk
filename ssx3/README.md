SSX 3 Mountain - 2026 - Fly around the whole mountain from SSX 3, reverse engineered from the GameCube disc.

All three peaks of the game's world in one place, with its terrain, objects, textures, baked lighting and skies. The levels stream in as you get close, and you can jump to any course from the list of places.

Drag to move, right-drag or shift-drag to look around, scroll to zoom, and W A S D to fly. On a phone, drag with one finger, and pinch, twist or slide with two. Option-click or long-press on anything to see what it's made of.

`scripts/` is pure Python: it reads the disc image, decodes the world format, and writes the re-encoded `data/` files the page loads. `CLAUDE.md` has the notes on the file format.
